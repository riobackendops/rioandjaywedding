07 · NOTES FROM THE COUPLE

Good to know

By invitation only

We prefer meaningful connections over large crowds, so we're keeping our wedding intimate. No plus ones, please. If you're receiving this, thank you for being one of those people.

Unplugged ceremony

Please keep phones, cameras and devices away during the ceremony. Our photographers and videographers will capture every moment. Snap away at the reception and after party!

On gifts

Your love and presence are truly all we ask for. Should you wish to bless us, a monetary contribution toward our future would be sincerely appreciated. We'll hold a Prosperity Activity during the reception where gifts may be given.

Adults-only celebration

While we adore your little ones, our wedding is adults only. The only children attending will be those in the entourage.
