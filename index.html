01 · THE DAY

Where & when

The VFarm Bamban

Bamban, Tarlac · Sunday, December 20, 2026

Three spaces on one property, a short walk apart. Only the cocktail hour is outdoors. Parking is available on site.

Find your call time

All on December 20. These are the times we need you at the venue, not the times to leave home.
