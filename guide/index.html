01 · Dress code

What to wear

Start here — this is the one thing that needs a head start. Find your group below, then dress to its palette so the room reads as one.

Ninongs & ninangs

Deep redBeigeDark beigeBlack
  • Long gown for the ninangs, suit and tie for the ninongs
  • Lace or chiffon preferred — a shawl or wrap works beautifully
  • Suits in taupe, beige, deep red or black

Guests

Dark beigeBeigeLight taupe
  • Formal is preferred, semi-formal is welcome
  • A gown, a dress or dressy trousers all work
  • A jacket is optional — a long-sleeved top and slacks is plenty
  • Cocktail hour is on grass, so pick shoes that can handle a lawn

Bridesmaids, groomsmen, flower girls & boys

Deep redBlackBeige

02 · Important reminders

Four things to know

The first two decide who can walk through the door with you. Please take a moment with all four before the day.

Strictly enforced

No plus ones

Only the people Rio and Jay invited by name. This is a private event, so anyone not on the guest list will not be able to enter. Every seat, every meal, every booth and every cup is already accounted for.

Strictly enforced

No kids

An adults-only celebration. The only children there will be the ones walking in our entourage.

If you wish to give

Monetary gifts

Your presence is genuinely the gift. If you would like to give something, a monetary gift helps us most — hand it to us during the Prosperity Activity at the reception.

Two minutes only

Phones down for the aisle

Cameras down during the bridal walk ONLY — entrance and exit. That is the one stretch our photo team cannot re-shoot. Everything else, all night, is yours.

Staying the night before?

Coming from Metro Manila, Davao or overseas, that is a long trip to make on the morning itself. The nearest hotels are in Clark / Mabalacat, 30–40 minutes away — Clark Marriott, Hilton Clark Sun Valley, Swissôtel Clark, Quest Plus or Midori — or an Airbnb in Bamban itself, usually only 15–20 minutes from the venue. We have not blocked rooms anywhere, so please book your own, and book early.

04 · When to arrive

Find your call time

All on December 20. These are the times we need you at the venue, not the times to leave home.

7:00 AM

Bridesmaids & groomsmen

You need to be at the venue by 7:00 AM for preparations. That is an early start, so Rio & Jay are providing overnight accommodation for you at the venue — come in on December 19, check-in runs 3:00 PM to 9:00 PM.

10:00 AM

Parents of the bride & groom

Please be at the venue by 10:00 AM for hair and make-up, and for the family photoshoot with the couple.

3:30 PM

Ninongs, ninangs & guests

Everyone outside the morning call times: be at the venue by 3:30 PM, so you have time to park, walk in and find your seat.
